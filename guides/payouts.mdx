Settled payments accumulate in one balance in your **[Account Currency](/guides/settlement)** — CHF unless you chose EUR, USD or GBP — which you pay out manually or on a schedule. Balances and payouts live in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.qint.ch).

## Balances

Every settled payment is credited to your balance in your Account Currency at the rate fixed when the buyer was quoted: the invoiced amount minus Qint's fee where you bear it — or, for a payment settled [underpaid](/guides/underpayments), the amount actually received minus Qint's fee on that amount. How the credit is computed is explained in [settlement and your Account Currency](/guides/settlement). The payment's detail view and the CSV export show the amount credited (`credit_amount`), its currency (`credit_currency`) and the rate (`credit_rate`).

The balance figure in your Account Currency is exact. The dashboard can also show it in a **display currency** of your choice; that second figure is indicative, at the live rate. The balance splits in two:

| Bucket | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Funds from payments settled within the holding period. Not yet withdrawable. |
| **Available** | Funds past the holding period, minus everything reserved by non-cancelled payouts. Withdrawable. |

The **holding period** is 1–7 days (default 2), set on your account by Qint. Requested and scheduled payouts reserve the amount they need from the available balance immediately, in your Account Currency — you can never request more than is available.

## Manual payouts

**Dashboard → Payouts → Withdraw funds.** Choose the payout type, the payout currency (CHF, EUR, USD or GBP — whichever Qint currently offers, see [currency availability](/accept-payments/api-payments#currency-availability)), the amount in that currency, and an optional note. Requires the Owner, Admin or Finance role.

- **In your Account Currency:** no conversion. The amount is debited from your balance as it is.
- **In another currency:** the amount is converted at the market rate at the time of your request. The dialog shows the rate and the amount that will be debited from your balance; the rate is fixed on the payout when you request it. There is **no conversion fee**: the [payout fees](#payout-fees) of the price sheet are the only charge.

The dashboard's payout quote (`GET /api/v1/merchant/payouts/quote`, signed-in session) carries these figures in its `fx` block: `sourceAmount` (what the payout debits, in your Account Currency), `available` (your available balance), `currency` (your Account Currency), `sourceRate` (payout-currency units per 1 Account Currency unit, `1` when they are the same) and `asOf` (the time of the rate).

| Type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Bank** | Transfer to the bank account from your onboarding profile. |
| **Cash** | Paid out in cash by the execution partner. Available, within a platform-wide daily limit of CHF 200'000 (see below). |
| **Crypto** | Not offered at launch. The dashboard shows it as not available, and a request is refused with HTTP 409 and `code: "payout_method_disabled"`. See [payout wallets](#payout-wallets). |

Cash payouts share a platform-wide daily limit of CHF 200'000 (equivalent) per calendar day in Europe/Zurich time, across all merchants and counting every requested, scheduled or executed cash payout of that day. A cash request (or its quote) that would exceed what is left today is refused with HTTP 409 and `code: "cash_daily_limit"`, whose message says how much is still available — request a smaller amount, try again the next day, or choose a bank payout.

Qint payouts are **record-and-track**: the payout request is registered with Qint's payment partner for execution, and its status updates flow back into your dashboard automatically. Each payout carries a reference once executed — use it to reconcile against your bank statement.

### Payout lifecycle

```text
Requested ──► Scheduled ──► Executed
     │             │
     └─────────────┴──► Cancelled  (releases the reserved funds)
```

A payout request that can't reach the payment partner immediately is retried automatically in the background — it stays visible as Requested with a sync-pending marker until confirmed.

## Payout wallets

<Note>
Crypto payouts are **not offered at launch**. The payout wallet flow below is kept for a later date and may be hidden in the dashboard until then; while crypto payouts are not offered, a crypto payout request is refused.
</Note>

When crypto payouts are offered, the amount is entered in your Account Currency and converted at the market rate at the time of the request. Crypto payouts are executed through the execution partner's custody (Fireblocks), which only sends to **whitelisted** addresses. You therefore register your wallets in advance, and a crypto payout can only go to a wallet that 24crypto has approved.

1. **Register.** Under **Dashboard → Settings → Payout wallets**, add a wallet: the network (Tron, Ethereum or Solana, as offered for the payout asset), the address and an optional label. The address format is checked for the network. Adding and removing wallets requires the Owner or Admin role.
2. **Approval.** Qint forwards the wallet to 24crypto, where it shows as **pending**. A 24crypto operator whitelists the address in Fireblocks and approves it — or rejects it with a note. Owner and Admin users get an e-mail either way ("Payout wallet approved" / "Payout wallet rejected", with the operator's note).
3. **Pay out.** In **Payouts → Withdraw funds → Crypto**, pick one of your approved wallets. The dialog lists approved wallets only.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **pending** | Registered, waiting for 24crypto to whitelist and approve it. Not usable yet. |
| **approved** | Whitelisted — crypto payouts can go to it. |
| **rejected** | Not approved (the note says why). An approved wallet can also be **withdrawn** later and then shows as rejected. |
| **removed** | You removed it. Removal is final: to use the address again, add it again — it starts as a new pending wallet. |

Payouts already requested are not affected by a later status change.

### Errors

| HTTP | `code` | When | Message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 409 | `wallet_not_whitelisted` | A crypto payout (or its quote) names a wallet that is not approved, or an address that is not one of your approved wallets. The response also carries `walletId` and `walletStatus` when a matching wallet exists. | e.g. "This TRON address isn't an approved payout wallet. Add it under Settings → Payout wallets and wait until 24crypto has approved it — crypto payouts only go to approved wallets." — or, for a wallet still under review, "This payout wallet is still waiting for 24crypto's approval. Crypto payouts only go to approved wallets — check its status under Settings → Payout wallets." |
| 409 | `wallet_already_registered` | You add an address that is already one of your pending or approved wallets on that network. | "This TRON address is already one of your payout wallets." |

A crypto payout request references its wallet with `payoutWalletId` (the wallet's `pw_…` id); `network` and `destinationAddress` are then taken from the wallet. Requests that send `network` and `destinationAddress` without an id are still accepted, but only when they match an approved wallet.

## Scheduled payouts

<Note>
Automatic payouts are switched on per merchant by Qint. Until that happens your
schedule is **Manual** — you request each payout yourself — and the dashboard
shows "Automatic payouts off" instead of the schedule picker. Contact support to
have scheduled payouts enabled for your account.
</Note>

Prefer a standing sweep? The payout schedule on **Dashboard → Payouts** offers:

| Schedule | Runs |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Every day |
| Weekly | Mondays |
| Monthly | The 1st of the month |

When due, Qint automatically creates one **Bank** payout of the full available balance in your schedule currency (or your bank currency when none is set, else your Account Currency), converted at the market rate of that moment only when it differs from your Account Currency — provided it's at least **10.00** in that currency. On the payout routes with a minimum (see [Fees](#fees)), what happens below the equivalent of **CHF 5'000** depends on whether you have set a [minimum amount for automatic payouts](#minimum-amount-for-automatic-payouts):

- **No minimum set:** the scheduled payout waits until the balance is worth the equivalent of CHF 5'000 and is free. It never pays the early-payout fee; the balance simply accumulates until it reaches the threshold.
- **Minimum set:** the scheduled payout goes out as soon as the balance reaches your minimum, and a payout below the equivalent of CHF 5'000 is charged the CHF 25 early-payout fee of the price sheet, exactly as a manual payout of the same amount would be.

CHF payouts to a Swiss bank account have no threshold and are always free: they go out from 10.00. Configuring schedules requires the Owner or Admin role.

### Minimum amount for automatic payouts

You can set a **minimum balance for automatic payouts** under **Dashboard → Payouts**, in your Account Currency (two decimal places). A scheduled payout then runs as soon as your available balance reaches that amount; below it, nothing is paid out that period and the balance is checked again on the next due day. A value below 10.00 does not lower the 10.00 floor.

If you set a minimum balance, automatic payouts no longer wait for the CHF 5'000 threshold: on the routes with a minimum (EUR to a Swiss bank account, CHF to a foreign bank account, other foreign currencies), a payout below the equivalent of CHF 5'000 is charged **CHF 25**, deducted from the payout, as set out in the [price sheet](https://qint.ch/pricing#payout-fees). Setting the minimum counts as your consent to that fee; the payout row shows the fee and the net amount like any early payout. CHF payouts to a Swiss bank account are always free.

Leave the field empty for no minimum: automatic payouts on the routes with a minimum then wait until your balance reaches the equivalent of CHF 5'000 and are free. Manual payouts are not affected. Setting the minimum requires the Owner or Admin role.

## Fees

Qint's service fee is a small **conversion spread** (0.5 %) taken when a payment is credited to your balance — there are no fee lines on invoices or checkout, and a payout in a currency other than your Account Currency carries no conversion fee. Your current fee plan (spread and who pays it) is shown in the dashboard, where Owner/Admin users can also toggle **who pays it**: absorbed by you, or added on top for the customer.

**Underpayment tolerance:** 0.5 % by default, up to 2 % at your choice (Settings → Payouts). A crypto payment short by no more than the tolerance counts as paid in full and is credited on the amount received — see [underpayments](/guides/underpayments). The tolerance is listed with the fees on [qint.ch/pricing](https://qint.ch/pricing#underpayment-tolerance).

### Payout fees

Payout fees apply in addition to the conversion spread and depend on where the money goes. They are the "price sheet" referenced by the [Terms & Conditions](https://qint.ch/terms#fees) (version 2026-10), published at [qint.ch/pricing](https://qint.ch/pricing#payout-fees). Bank payouts below the equivalent of CHF 5'000 on the routes with a minimum are charged CHF 25 per payout, as set out in the [price sheet](https://qint.ch/pricing#payout-fees):

| Payout type | Minimum amount | Regular payout fee | Payout below CHF 5'000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CHF to a Swiss bank account** | None | None | Not applicable — no payout threshold |
| **EUR to a Swiss bank account (CH IBAN)** | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |
| **CHF to a foreign bank account** (e.g. Revolut) | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |
| **Any other foreign-currency payout** (e.g. USD or GBP) | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |

The route is decided per payout from the payout currency and the country of your registered IBAN. A GBP payout always falls under **any other foreign-currency payout**. For payout types with a minimum, your balance accumulates until a payout is worth the equivalent of CHF 5'000 (at the current CHF rate) — then it is free. If you need the money earlier, the dashboard shows the fee and the net amount before you confirm; the early-payout fee is deducted from the payout and the **net** amount is transferred. Payout rows and the CSV export carry `fee_amount` and `net_amount`; the requested gross amount is what is reserved from your balance.

<Note>
Requesting an early payout through the dashboard requires an explicit confirmation of the fee. Scheduled payouts charge it only if you have set a [minimum amount for automatic payouts](#minimum-amount-for-automatic-payouts); without one they wait for the threshold and are free.
</Note>
