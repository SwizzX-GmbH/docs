Settled payments accumulate as fiat balances — one per currency — which you pay out manually or on a schedule. Balances and payouts live in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.qint.ch).

## Balances

Every settled payment credits your balance in its currency. Each balance splits in two:

| Bucket | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Funds from payments settled within the holding period. Not yet withdrawable. |
| **Available** | Funds past the holding period, minus everything reserved by non-cancelled payouts. Withdrawable. |

The **holding period** is 1–3 days (default 2), set on your account by Qint. Requested and scheduled payouts reserve their amount from the available balance immediately — you can never request more than is available.

## Manual payouts

**Dashboard → Payouts → Request payout.** Choose the type, amount, currency, and an optional note. Requires the Owner, Admin or Finance role.

| Type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Bank** | Transfer to the bank account from your onboarding profile. |
| **Cash** | Disabled pending written compliance approval; not generally available. |
| **Crypto** | Requires an explicitly agreed, tested payout arrangement with the execution partner; not a launch-ready self-service workflow. |

Qint payouts are **record-and-track**: the payout request is registered with Qint's payment partner for execution, and its status updates flow back into your dashboard automatically. Each payout carries a reference once executed — use it to reconcile against your bank statement.

### Payout lifecycle

```text
Requested ──► Scheduled ──► Executed
     │             │
     └─────────────┴──► Cancelled  (releases the reserved funds)
```

A payout request that can't reach the payment partner immediately is retried automatically in the background — it stays visible as Requested with a sync-pending marker until confirmed.

## Scheduled payouts

Prefer a standing sweep? **Dashboard → Payout settings** offers:

| Schedule | Runs |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Every day |
| Weekly | Mondays |
| Monthly | The 1st of the month |

When due, Qint automatically creates a **Bank** payout of the full available balance per currency — provided it's at least **10.00** in that currency and, for the payout types with a minimum (see [Fees](#fees)), worth at least the equivalent of **CHF 5'000**. Scheduled payouts never pay the early-payout fee: the balance simply accumulates until it reaches the threshold. You can scope the schedule to a single currency or let it sweep all of them. Configuring schedules requires the Owner or Admin role.

## Fees

Qint's service fee is a small **conversion spread** applied when crypto is converted to your settlement currency — there are no fee lines on invoices or checkout. Your current fee plan (spread and who pays it) is shown in the dashboard, where Owner/Admin users can also toggle **who pays it**: absorbed by you, or added on top for the customer.

### Payout fees

Payout fees apply in addition to the conversion spread and depend on where the money goes. They are the "price sheet" referenced by the [Terms & Conditions](https://qint.ch/terms#fees) (version 2026-10):

| Payout type | Minimum amount | Regular payout fee | Payout below CHF 5'000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CHF to a Swiss bank account** | None | None | Not applicable — no payout threshold |
| **EUR to a Swiss bank account (CH IBAN)** | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |
| **CHF to a foreign bank account** (e.g. Revolut) | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |
| **Any other foreign-currency payout** | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |

The route is decided per payout from the payout currency and the country of your registered IBAN. For payout types with a minimum, your balance accumulates until a payout is worth the equivalent of CHF 5'000 (at the current CHF rate) — then it is free. If you need the money earlier, the dashboard shows the fee and the net amount before you confirm; the early-payout fee is deducted from the payout and the **net** amount is transferred. Payout rows and the CSV export carry `fee_amount` and `net_amount`; the requested gross amount is what is reserved from your balance.

<Note>
Requesting an early payout through the dashboard requires an explicit confirmation of the fee. Scheduled payouts never charge it — they wait for the threshold.
</Note>
