Settled payments accumulate in one balance held in a stablecoin — **USDT**, or **USDC** if your business is registered in the European Economic Area — which you pay out manually or on a schedule in CHF, EUR, USD or GBP. The dashboard shows the balance in the currency you choose. Balances and payouts live in the [dashboard](https://dashboard.qint.ch).

## Settlement asset

Your **settlement asset** is the stablecoin your balance is held in:

| Country of registration | Settlement asset | Crypto payouts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A state of the **European Economic Area** — the 27 EU member states, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway | **USDC** | USDC only — on Ethereum (ERC-20) or Solana |
| Anywhere else (including Switzerland and the UK) | **USDT** | USDT only — on Tron (TRC-20), Ethereum (ERC-20) or Solana |

USDT is not authorised under the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA), which applies across the EEA, so EEA merchants settle in USDC. The asset is set from the country of registration you enter during onboarding. It is shown on **Dashboard → Balances**, under **Settings → Payouts**, and in the API as `creditAsset` on a payment intent ([API payments](/accept-payments/api-payments)). Your customers can still pay in any [accepted asset](/accept-payments/assets-and-networks); only what your balance is held in changes. Fees and bank payouts work the same for both. Qint support can change the asset only once your balance in the current asset has been paid out — funds are never converted between stablecoins by a settings change.

## Balances

Your balance is kept in your settlement asset (below, "USDT" stands for either). When a payment settles, the invoiced amount minus Qint's fee — or, for a payment settled [underpaid](/guides/underpayments), the amount actually received minus Qint's fee on that amount — is converted into the settlement asset at the market rate of that moment and credited; the payment's detail view and the CSV export show the amount credited, the asset (`credit_asset`) and the rate used. The dashboard displays the balance in your **display currency** (CHF, EUR, USD or GBP, chosen under Settings → Payouts) as a live estimate next to the exact USDT or USDC figure. The balance splits in two:

| Bucket | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Funds from payments settled within the holding period. Not yet withdrawable. |
| **Available** | Funds past the holding period, minus everything reserved by non-cancelled payouts. Withdrawable. |

The **holding period** is 1–7 days (default 2), set on your account by Qint. Requested and scheduled payouts reserve the USDT they need from the available balance immediately — you can never request more than is available.

## Manual payouts

**Dashboard → Payouts → Withdraw funds.** Choose the payout type (bank, cash or crypto), the payout currency (CHF, EUR, USD or GBP — whichever Qint currently offers, see [currency availability](/accept-payments/api-payments#currency-availability)), the amount in that currency, and an optional note. The dialog shows the live market rate and the USDT that will be sold; the rate is frozen on the payout when you request it, and there is no conversion fee. Requires the Owner, Admin or Finance role.

| Type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Bank** | Transfer to the bank account from your onboarding profile. |
| **Cash** | Paid out in cash by the execution partner. Available, within a platform-wide daily limit of CHF 200'000 (see below). |
| **Crypto** | Sent on-chain to one of your **approved** [payout wallets](#payout-wallets) — never to an address typed in at request time. Paid in your [settlement asset](#settlement-asset) only — USDC for EEA merchants, USDT otherwise; a request in the other stablecoin is refused. |

Cash payouts share a platform-wide daily limit of CHF 200'000 (equivalent) per calendar day in Europe/Zurich time, across all merchants and counting every requested, scheduled or executed cash payout of that day. A cash request (or its quote) that would exceed what is left today is refused with HTTP 409 and `code: "cash_daily_limit"`, whose message says how much is still available — request a smaller amount, try again the next day, or choose bank or crypto.

Qint payouts are **record-and-track**: the payout request is registered with Qint's payment partner for execution, and its status updates flow back into your dashboard automatically. Each payout carries a reference once executed — use it to reconcile against your bank statement.

### Payout lifecycle

```text
Requested ──► Scheduled ──► Executed
     │             │
     └─────────────┴──► Cancelled  (releases the reserved funds)
```

A payout request that can't reach the payment partner immediately is retried automatically in the background — it stays visible as Requested with a sync-pending marker until confirmed.

## Payout wallets

Crypto payouts are executed through the execution partner's custody (Fireblocks), which only sends to **whitelisted** addresses. You therefore register your wallets in advance, and a crypto payout can only go to a wallet that 24crypto has approved.

1. **Register.** Under **Dashboard → Settings → Payout wallets**, add a wallet: the network (one of the networks of your [settlement asset](#settlement-asset) — Tron, Ethereum or Solana for USDT; Ethereum or Solana for USDC), the address and an optional label. The address format is checked for the network. Adding and removing wallets requires the Owner or Admin role.
2. **Approval.** Qint forwards the wallet to 24crypto, where it shows as **pending**. A 24crypto operator whitelists the address in Fireblocks and approves it — or rejects it with a note. Owner and Admin users get an e-mail either way ("Payout wallet approved" / "Payout wallet rejected", with the operator's note).
3. **Pay out.** In **Payouts → Withdraw funds → Crypto**, pick one of your approved wallets. The dialog lists approved wallets only.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **pending** | Registered, waiting for 24crypto to whitelist and approve it. Not usable yet. |
| **approved** | Whitelisted — crypto payouts can go to it. |
| **rejected** | Not approved (the note says why). An approved wallet can also be **withdrawn** later and then shows as rejected. |
| **removed** | You removed it. Removal is final: to use the address again, add it again — it starts as a new pending wallet. |

Payouts already requested are not affected by a later status change.

### Errors

| HTTP | `code` | When | Message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 409 | `wallet_not_whitelisted` | A crypto payout (or its quote) names a wallet that is not approved, or an address that is not one of your approved wallets. The response also carries `walletId` and `walletStatus` when a matching wallet exists. | e.g. "This TRON address isn't an approved payout wallet. Add it under Settings → Payout wallets and wait until 24crypto has approved it — crypto payouts only go to approved wallets." — or, for a wallet still under review, "This payout wallet is still waiting for 24crypto's approval. Crypto payouts only go to approved wallets — check its status under Settings → Payout wallets." |
| 409 | `wallet_already_registered` | You add an address that is already one of your pending or approved wallets on that network. | "This TRON address is already one of your payout wallets." |

A crypto payout request references its wallet with `payoutWalletId` (the wallet's `pw_…` id); `network` and `destinationAddress` are then taken from the wallet. Requests that send `network` and `destinationAddress` without an id are still accepted, but only when they match an approved wallet.

## Scheduled payouts

<Note>
Automatic payouts are switched on per merchant by Qint. Until that happens your
schedule is **Manual** — you request each payout yourself — and the dashboard
shows "Automatic payouts off" instead of the schedule picker. Contact support to
have scheduled payouts enabled for your account.
</Note>

Prefer a standing sweep? **Dashboard → Payout settings** offers:

| Schedule | Runs |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Every day |
| Weekly | Mondays |
| Monthly | The 1st of the month |

When due, Qint automatically creates one **Bank** payout of the full available balance, converted into your schedule currency (or your bank currency when none is set) — provided it's at least **10.00** in that currency and, for the payout routes with a minimum (see [Fees](#fees)), worth at least the equivalent of **CHF 5'000**. Scheduled payouts never pay the early-payout fee: the balance simply accumulates until it reaches the threshold. Configuring schedules requires the Owner or Admin role.

## Fees

Qint's service fee is a small **conversion spread** taken when a payment is credited to your balance — there are no fee lines on invoices or checkout, and converting your balance to CHF, EUR, USD or GBP at payout carries no additional fee. Your current fee plan (spread and who pays it) is shown in the dashboard, where Owner/Admin users can also toggle **who pays it**: absorbed by you, or added on top for the customer.

**Underpayment tolerance:** 0.5 % by default, up to 2 % at your choice (Settings → Payouts). A crypto payment short by no more than the tolerance counts as paid in full and is credited on the amount received — see [underpayments](/guides/underpayments). The tolerance is listed with the fees on [qint.ch/pricing](https://qint.ch/pricing#underpayment-tolerance).

### Payout fees

Payout fees apply in addition to the conversion spread and depend on where the money goes. They are the "price sheet" referenced by the [Terms & Conditions](https://qint.ch/terms#fees) (version 2026-10):

| Payout type | Minimum amount | Regular payout fee | Payout below CHF 5'000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CHF to a Swiss bank account** | None | None | Not applicable — no payout threshold |
| **EUR to a Swiss bank account (CH IBAN)** | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |
| **CHF to a foreign bank account** (e.g. Revolut) | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |
| **Any other foreign-currency payout** (e.g. USD or GBP) | Equivalent of CHF 5'000 | Free once the minimum is reached | **CHF 25** per early payout, deducted from the payout |

The route is decided per payout from the payout currency and the country of your registered IBAN. A GBP payout always falls under **any other foreign-currency payout**. For payout types with a minimum, your balance accumulates until a payout is worth the equivalent of CHF 5'000 (at the current CHF rate) — then it is free. If you need the money earlier, the dashboard shows the fee and the net amount before you confirm; the early-payout fee is deducted from the payout and the **net** amount is transferred. Payout rows and the CSV export carry `fee_amount` and `net_amount`; the requested gross amount is what is reserved from your balance.

<Note>
Requesting an early payout through the dashboard requires an explicit confirmation of the fee. Scheduled payouts never charge it — they wait for the threshold.
</Note>
